Durban Farmers' Market Online is a community-focused web platform designed for a Durban-based marketplace in KwaZulu-Natal. The project addresses information fragmentation—where vendor lists and operating dates are scattered across social media—by establishing a single, accessible digital directory. 

The website connects local households, health-conscious shoppers, and small businesses directly with regional farmers, artisanal bakers, and craft producers. It enables visitors to explore available market goods, view upcoming operating dates across primary and secondary locations, and submit pre-order collection list enquiries prior to visiting on market day.
